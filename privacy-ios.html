Privacy Policy — London Tube AI for iPhone and iPad

Last updated: 31 October 2026

The short version. London Tube AI for iPhone and iPad has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service, and it does not track you. It sends only what a feature needs to the transport and weather services that answer it, and everything it remembers stays on your device. We do not run a server, so we receive nothing from the app. The only personal data we ever receive is an email you choose to send us. This page covers the iPhone and iPad app. The Android app has its own privacy policy.

1. Who we are

London Tube AI is published by TubeAI Labs, a developer based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law, TubeAI Labs is the data controller for this app. Contact: londontubenavigator@gmail.com

London Tube AI is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Transport for London, the Department for Transport, Apple, or any transport operator.

2. What the app uses, and why

Location (optional)

The app asks for location only when you choose a feature that needs it, such as Nearby, planning from your current location, or tracking a journey. It uses your location to:

  • find stations and stops near you and work out walking distance;
  • plan a journey from where you are;
  • follow a journey you have started, including while your device is locked, until you pause or end it.

When a feature needs it, your coordinate is included in the request to Transport for London (section 3). The app does not keep your coordinates in its journey history and does not build a record of your movements. You can refuse or change location access, including precise location, at any time in iOS Settings; you can still plan journeys by choosing stations or places yourself.

Notifications (optional)

If you turn on disruption alerts, the app checks TfL line status on your device, at most twice a day, and notifies you. If you track a journey, it can alert you before your stop. These notifications are created on your device. There is no notification server and no marketing messages.

Live Activities, widgets and calendar

Journey progress on the Lock Screen and the Home Screen widgets is produced on your device from the same data the app already holds. "Add to calendar" opens Apple's own event editor with the journey filled in; the app cannot read your calendar.

Optional tips

Every feature is free. You can choose to leave a tip from Settings. Apple processes the payment under its own terms; the app receives only Apple's confirmation that the tip completed, and does not store, send or share any payment or purchase details. A tip unlocks nothing. We do not receive your name or payment details from Apple. Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

3. Services the app contacts

  • Transport for London (TfL) — line status, arrivals, timetables, fares, crowding, nearby stops, lift status, cycle docks and journey planning. A route or nearby request can include the station, place or coordinate needed to answer it. TfL privacy information.
  • Department for Transport — Bus Open Data Service — live bus positions on the map. The request contains the area of the map you are looking at, not your location.
  • MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute) — the weather on the Home screen. The request uses a fixed London-area location, not your device location. As MET Norway requires, the request names the app, its version and our contact address; it carries nothing about you. MET Norway terms.
  • Apple — maps are drawn with Apple MapKit, tips are processed by Apple's In-App Purchase, and app ratings use Apple's standard review prompt. Apple privacy policy.

Like any internet request, these services can see your device's IP address. We do not receive it, and we do not operate a server.

4. What stays on your device

Saved places and stations, favourites, recent searches, completed journeys you confirm, route preferences, cached transport data and widget data are stored only on your device. "Personalisation" in Settings controls whether the app reads your completed journeys to tailor what it shows; with it off, they are not read. You can export it (Settings → Data & storage → Export my data) or delete it (Settings → Data & storage → Delete saved app data) in the app, or delete it all by deleting the app.

5. If you email us

"Report a problem" and "Send feedback" open your Mail app with a message addressed to us. The subject carries the app's version; a report sent from a journey also includes that journey's stations, lines and times, so it can be checked against what TfL said at the time. You can read and change the message before sending it. If you send it, we receive your email address and what you wrote, use them only to reply and to fix the problem, and keep the conversation only as long as that takes.

6. What the app does not do

  • No accounts or sign-in.
  • No advertising, no advertising identifier, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting service.
  • No subscriptions, and nothing locked behind a payment.
  • No sale or sharing of personal data with data brokers.

7. Your rights under UK data protection law

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its use, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because the app has no accounts and we operate no servers, the only personal data we can hold about you is an email you sent us; ask and we will send you a copy or delete it. Deleting the app's data, or the app, erases everything it kept on your device immediately. For any question, email us and we will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint.

8. Children

London Tube AI is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Changes will be posted here with a new "Last updated" date, and a change to how data is used will be explained in the app before it takes effect.
