Data Deletion Request

This page explains how users of London Tube AI can delete their data. The app has no accounts, no sign-up and no purchases, so almost everything it keeps is stored only on your own device.

Last updated: 7 October 2026

Delete the data on your device

Your saved places, favourites, recent searches, journey history, cached routes and settings are stored only on your device. To delete them:

  • In the app: Settings → delete or reset your app data.
  • Android: Settings → Apps → London Tube AI → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • iPhone: delete the app from your Home Screen, which removes its data and widget data.

Delete data held by service providers

On Android, if you chose to turn on anonymous analytics or crash reporting, Google Firebase may hold records linked to a random app-instance identifier. The iOS app contains no analytics or crash-reporting service.

To ask us to request deletion of those records, email londontubenavigator@gmail.com with the subject Delete London Tube AI Data. Turning analytics and crash reporting off in the app stops any further collection immediately.

We can also delete any support emails you have sent us.

What may be kept

  • Aggregated or anonymised statistics that no longer identify a person or device.
  • Records we are required to keep by law, if any apply.

The app sells nothing, so there are no payment, subscription or billing records.

How long it takes

We aim to complete deletion requests within 30 days of receiving enough information to process them. If we need more information, we will reply to the email address you used.

Accounts

London Tube AI does not offer accounts or login, so there is no account to delete.

Contact

For data deletion requests or privacy questions, contact: londontubenavigator@gmail.com
