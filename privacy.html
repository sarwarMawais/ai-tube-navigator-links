Privacy Policy

London Tube AI (also published as AI Tube Navigator)
Last updated: 1 August 2026

The short version. London Tube AI has no accounts and no sign-up. We operate no server that receives or stores your data. Your journeys, favourites and history stay on your phone.

Analytics and crash reporting are switched off by default — nothing is collected unless you turn it on. The app contains no advertising and no tracking SDKs. We do not sell your data and we never share it with data brokers.

To work, the app sends your coordinates to Transport for London, Open-Meteo and Google Maps at the moment a feature needs them. None of them receive your name or a record of your movements.

1. Who we are

London Tube AI is published by TubeAI Labs, a developer based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of UK data protection law, TubeAI Labs is the data controller for this app.

Contact: londontubenavigator@gmail.com

London Tube AI is an independent app. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or connected to Transport for London, the Mayor of London, the Greater London Authority, or any transport operator.

2. What the app collects, and why

Location

If you grant location permission, the app uses your approximate and precise location to:

  • Find Tube, rail and bus stops near you
  • Show your position on the map
  • Calculate walking distance to nearby stops
  • Track your progress during a live journey and alert you before your stop
  • Show weather at your destination

Location permission is optional. If you decline it you can still plan journeys by typing station names, check line status, and use every other part of the app.

During a live journey the app runs a foreground service with location access, so it can keep counting stops while your screen is off or you are using another app. It runs only while a journey is active, it displays a persistent notification for the entire time it is running, and you can stop it at any point. The app does not request background location permission and cannot access your location when no journey is running.

We never store your location, because we have nowhere to store it.

Analytics, crash reporting and performance monitoring

These are switched off by default. Nothing is collected unless you turn them on in the app's privacy settings, and you can turn them off again at any time.

When you enable them, Firebase collects screen views, feature usage, route planning events, crash logs, diagnostics, app performance metrics, and a randomly generated app instance identifier. They do not collect your name, email address, phone number, or contacts.

Personalisation

The app offers an optional Personalisation setting that learns your regular journeys in order to suggest better routes and surface your usual commute more quickly.

This runs entirely on your device. Your journey patterns are analysed locally and are never uploaded, transmitted, or shared with us or anyone else. Nothing about your commute leaves your phone.

Because it involves no sharing, Personalisation is on by default. You can turn it off at any time in the app's privacy settings, and clearing app storage deletes everything it has learned.

Purchases

If you buy premium features, Google Play Billing processes the transaction and tells the app which features to unlock. Your card details are handled entirely by Google and are never seen by us.

What is stored on your device

Journey search history, saved routes, favourite stations, route preferences, notification settings, cached timetable data and theme choice are stored locally using Android's DataStore and a Room database, inside the app's private storage. None of it is uploaded. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling deletes it permanently.

3. Who receives your data

The app sends data to the following services. This is the complete list.

ServiceWhat it receivesWhy
Transport for London Your coordinates when you search for nearby stops; the stations you are travelling between when you plan a journey To return nearby stops, routes, live arrivals and line status
Open-Meteo Your coordinates when weather is shown To return the forecast for that location
Google Maps Platform Location and device data needed to render the map To display the map and your position on it
Google Play Billing Purchase and subscription status To process purchases and unlock premium features
Google Firebase Usage events, crash logs, diagnostics, app instance ID — only if you switch them on To diagnose crashes and understand which features are used

These services receive coordinates and technical data only. They do not receive your name, your email address, or any stored history of your movements from us.

We do not sell your data. We do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. The app contains no advertising SDK and no third-party tracking SDK of any kind.

5. Your choices

  • Location: grant, deny or revoke at any time in Android Settings → Apps → London Tube AI → Permissions
  • Notifications: the same place, or in the app's Settings screen
  • Analytics and crash reporting: off by default; turn on or off in the app's privacy settings
  • Personalisation: on by default because nothing is shared; turn it off in the same place
  • Everything stored on your device: clear the app's storage, or uninstall

6. Your rights under UK data protection law

You have the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to how it is used, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time.

Because we hold no accounts and operate no servers, we hold almost no personal data about you. Most of what the app knows lives only on your phone, where you control it directly — clearing app storage or uninstalling exercises your right to erasure immediately and completely. For anything held by the third-party services in Section 3, you can exercise your rights with them directly using the links above, or contact us and we will help.

To make a request, email londontubenavigator@gmail.com or use the Data Deletion Request page. We will respond within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data, you can complain to the UK Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk/make-a-complaint or on 0303 123 1113.

7. International transfers

Everything stored on your device stays on your device and is never transferred anywhere.

Transport for London processes data in the United Kingdom. Google and Open-Meteo may process data outside the UK, including in the United States, under the safeguards set out in their own privacy policies, which are linked in Section 3.

8. How long data is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it, clear app storage, or uninstall
  • By us: we operate no server, so we retain nothing
  • By third parties: according to their own retention policies, linked in Section 3
  • Purchase records: retained by Google Play, and by us where required for tax, accounting, refund or fraud-prevention purposes

9. Security

  • All data leaving the app is encrypted in transit using HTTPS/TLS
  • Unencrypted HTTP traffic is blocked at the network configuration level and cannot occur
  • Data stored on your device sits in Android's private application sandbox, inaccessible to other apps
  • Google Consent Mode v2 defaults to denied for every consent type until you choose otherwise
  • There is no central database of user journeys to be breached, because we do not have one

10. Children

London Tube AI is intended for users aged 13 and over. It is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. Material changes will be posted here with a revised "Last updated" date, and where a change affects how your data is used we will tell you in the app before it takes effect. Where a change requires your consent, we will ask for it rather than assume it.
